FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Ava a real person?

No. Ava is an AI career coach. She gives practical, specific guidance based on what you share, but it's not legal, financial or mental-health advice, and she can't promise jobs or salaries.

Do I have to upload my resume?

No. You can choose "Tell us about you" and type your current role, experience and the role you want. A resume lets Ava ask sharper questions, but it's optional.

What happens after the call?

Within a few minutes you get an email with your goal, gaps to close, a 30-day plan, resume tips and hand-picked resources: videos, courses only where they help, and senior people to connect with.

Will Ava remember me next time?

Yes. Enter the same number and Ava continues from your last call. You can download or delete everything on the My data page.

Which language does Ava speak?

English. You can speak in Hindi or Hinglish and Ava will understand, but she replies in English.

How is my data protected?

Your number is verified by a one-time code, data is stored in Google Cloud in Mumbai, and nothing is sold or used for ads. Read the privacy policy.
