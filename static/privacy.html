Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-25. This policy explains how Disha, the AI career coach, handles your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Who is responsible for your data

The Data Fiduciary is Alok Ranjan, an individual running Disha in India. For any question, request or complaint about your data, contact the Grievance Officer: Alok Ranjan, ranjanalok2032@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days and resolve complaints within 30 days.

What we collect, and why

DataPurpose
Mobile number (verified by one-time code)To confirm it's your number, place the coaching call, recognise you on future calls, and send the WhatsApp summary if you choose.
Name and emailTo greet you on the call and email your summary.
Resume textSo the coach can ask questions and give advice specific to your experience.
Call audio (processed live, not recorded) and the call transcriptTo run the coaching conversation, remember it on your next call, send your summary, and review coaching quality.
Coaching summary: goal, gaps, plan, resourcesTo send you your summary and continue from it on later calls.
Payment details (handled by Razorpay; we keep only the payment ID, amount and date)To give you a day pass and meet tax record-keeping law.
Consent records: what you agreed to, when, IP address and browserTo prove your consent, as the DPDP Act requires.

We collect only what is needed for these purposes. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Resources we suggest are not promotions.

Your consent

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give on the website before each call. You can withdraw consent at any time on the My data page by deleting your data. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing already done.

Who processes your data for us

  • Google Cloud (servers and storage in Mumbai, India) and Google Gemini (the AI that runs the conversation and research). Gemini processing may take place outside India.
  • Google Identity Platform: sends and checks the one-time code.
  • Vobiz: places the phone call.
  • Razorpay: processes payments.
  • Twilio and Meta (WhatsApp): deliver the WhatsApp summary, only if you opt in.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Google Sheets): sends your email summary and keeps call records for quality review.

Transfers outside India are only to countries not restricted by the Government of India under the DPDP Act.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile, resume and call transcripts: deleted automatically 12 months after your last call, or immediately when you delete your data.
  • Temporary call requests: deleted within 1 day.
  • Payment records: kept as long as tax law requires, with only a coded form of your number.
  • A count of free calls used, under a coded form of your number, is kept after deletion so free calls can't be claimed again. It contains no other data.

Your rights

  • Access: download everything we store about you on the My data page.
  • Correction: update your name, email or resume on the home page before your next call, or email the Grievance Officer.
  • Erasure: delete your data on the My data page.
  • Grievance redressal: contact the Grievance Officer above. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nomination: you may nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity, by emailing the Grievance Officer.

Security

Data is stored in private, access-controlled Google Cloud storage and encrypted in transit and at rest. Call links are single-use. If a breach affects your data, we'll inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Children

Disha is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly process children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the version date above and ask for your consent again where the law requires.
